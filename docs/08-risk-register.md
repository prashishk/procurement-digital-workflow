# 08 — Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambiguous requirements | High | structured intake + validation | Product |
| Incorrect approval routing | High | rule-based matrix + UAT | Product/Tech |
| AI hallucination | High | retrieval/constraints + human approval | Tech |
| Sensitive data exposure | Critical | minimisation + RBAC + encryption | Security |
| Integration failure | High | retries, monitoring, reconciliation | Tech |
| User adoption friction | Medium | workflow usability + training | Product |
| Audit evidence gaps | High | event model + mandatory evidence | Product/Tech |

## Delivery RAID

The delivery team should maintain a live **Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies** register, with explicit owner, due date, mitigation and escalation path.
