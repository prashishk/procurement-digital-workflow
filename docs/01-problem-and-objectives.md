# 01 — Problem & Objectives

## Business Problem

Procurement in a regulated environment is not a single transaction. It is a chain of handoffs involving requirements, specifications, commercial controls, tender publication, evaluation, approvals and exceptions.

The transformation objective is to make that chain **visible, controlled and traceable** without removing accountable human decision-makers.

## Objectives

1. Standardise the end-to-end procurement workflow.
2. Make ownership and approval gates explicit.
3. Maintain a complete decision and evidence trail.
4. Reduce avoidable rework caused by incomplete or inconsistent submissions.
5. Create a foundation for controlled automation and analytics.

## Success Measures

- % submissions passing validation at first review
- cycle time by workflow stage
- approval turnaround time
- rework / return rate
- SLA breach rate
- exception rate and resolution time
- % records with complete audit evidence

## Scope

**In:** requirements, specifications, RFP preparation, review, tender publication handoff, evaluation workflow, approvals, exceptions and auditability.

**Out:** autonomous procurement decisions, confidential production data and direct representation of any government system's source code.
